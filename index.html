Guidance, not a score

Development follows abilities, not birthdays

Infancy covers birth to 12 months, toddlerhood 12–36 months, and early-childhood browsing starts at three years. These overlapping transition ages organize activities; they are not biological tests. Follow current movement, communication, learning and regulation abilities.

Children develop at different rates. Crawling is not a required step before walking. Puberty and brain development vary between individuals; adulthood does not begin biologically on a single birthday. Later-life activities should fit health and function.

School entry, marriage, work and retirement do not determine developmental readiness here. Piaget and Erikson describe theoretical frameworks, not validated biological cutoffs.

Early childhood: notice and ask

CDC groups early milestones into social-emotional, language/communication, cognitive, and movement/physical domains. This journal uses those categories to organize activities; it does not reproduce a screening test.

If your child has lost a skill, is not meeting milestones, or you have concerns, talk with their clinician and ask about developmental screening. Do not wait for this journal to flag a problem.

AAP recommends general developmental screening at 9, 18, and 30 months, and autism screening at 18 and 24 months, or whenever there is a concern.

Relationships are not a test

Follow the other person's cues and respect consent. Eye contact, a particular distance from a caregiver, a romantic relationship, or emotional disclosure are never requirements here.

The adult categories organize optional activities. They do not establish a universal sequence or score attachment security.

Movement is a whole-day picture

WHO guidance addresses activity, sedentary time, and age-specific needs. Choose activities that interest you; you do not need to complete every activity. These short suggestions are not a complete physical-activity plan and do not calculate whether recommendations are met.

Choose adaptations that suit your health and abilities. Discuss new vigorous activity, injury recovery, or fall concerns with a qualified professional.

Prematurity and individual needs

For babies born preterm, use adjusted (corrected) age for milestone comparisons through about two years with your clinician’s guidance. Browsing age stays manual here; the app does not calculate screening dates or medical eligibility.

Activities can be shortened, supported, or communicated through speech, signs, pictures, or assistive tools. Choose activities that fit current readiness.
